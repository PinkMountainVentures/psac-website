Your adventure, handled

The mountain
is right there.

Most people in Palm Springs never go up it. You can. Here's how it works.

You're in Palm Springs. The Coachella Valley is spread out below you, the pool is perfect, the restaurants are right. And somewhere between the first morning coffee and the second day, you look up.

The mountains are enormous. They're close. And there's a version of this trip that happens up there. Quieter, sharper, more yours. One that most visitors never find.

Not because it's hard to find. Because nobody handed them a way in.

How it works

We handle everything.
You bring yourself.

01

Tell us about your group

Who's coming, how you like to move, what you're after. A few questions. Five minutes. Everything else follows from your answers.

02

We select your trail

Not from a list you scroll through. From lived experience on these specific trails: the junctions, the views, the right time of day, the terrain that fits your group. The route is chosen for you.

03

Your gear arrives the evening before

A Gregory daypack, Leki trekking poles, two laser-engraved Hydro Flask bottles, and a first aid kit. Plus LMNT electrolytes, Rancho Meladuco dates, Blue Lizard mineral sunscreen, and a bandana to keep. Packed. Delivered. Waiting for you.

04

Your route is already on your phone

Your trail loads through RideWithGPS before you leave. Turn-by-turn navigation, waypoints, terrain detail. Works without cell service. No signal required.

05

You go

That's the whole morning. No planning. No rentals. No strangers on someone else's itinerary. Just your group and a trail that was chosen for you.

06

We pick everything up

When you're done, leave the gear. We take care of the rest.

Your route guide

The trail,
before you're on it.

The night before you go, your route guide is ready. It's built for your specific trail, your specific group, the time of year. It covers the terrain, the waypoints, what to expect at each one, and the moments worth slowing down for.

Works without cell service.
No bars required.

It loads to your phone through RideWithGPS. There's also a printed route card in your kit with waypoints and landmarks for the trail.

You'll know exactly where you're going before you take a single step.

Is this for you

You don't need
to be a hiker.

You need to want a morning that feels different from the rest of the trip.

Palm Springs Adventure Club was built for people who are curious about the mountain but don't have the gear, the knowledge, or the time to piece together a plan. People who want the experience without the research. Solo travelers, couples, families, and groups of up to twelve who don't want a guided tour with strangers on a route they didn't choose.

If you've looked up at the ridgeline and thought about it, this is for you.

Good to know

A few practical things.

When does the gear arrive?

The evening before your trail day. We deliver to your hotel front desk or property. You pick it up that evening or grab it on your way out in the morning.

What about the gear deposit?

A refundable hold is placed on your card the day before delivery. It is not a charge. It's released in full when the gear comes back complete. The hold is $65 per kit for Trail Guide.

Do I need any experience?

No. Your trail is selected based on how your group moves and what you're looking for. There are trails here that are genuinely easy and genuinely extraordinary. We'll find the right one.

What if I haven't booked my hotel yet?

Book your experience now. We'll collect your accommodation details separately once you have them. No rush.

What do I need to bring?

Hiking shoes or trail runners with good grip. Sun protection for your face and arms. Everything else is in your kit.

How many people can book together?

Up to twelve. Solo travelers, couples, families, friend groups. The trail is selected for whoever is coming.
